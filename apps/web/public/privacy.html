Global Login

Privacy Policy

Global Login by Peacock Projects · Effective 1 November 2026

Global Login is a team credential manager. It consists of the Global Login Chrome extension and the web dashboard at devlogin.peacockindia.in. This policy explains what data the service handles and why.

Who can use it

Access requires signing in with a Google account, and an administrator of the workspace must approve each new account before it can see any data.

Data we collect

  • Account information: your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture, received from Google when you sign in. Used only to identify you and manage access.
  • Credentials and notes your team saves: project names, website addresses, login usernames, passwords and secure notes. Passwords and note contents are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are stored on our server.
  • Preferences: per-project choices such as your default login and whether automatic sign-in is enabled.

How the extension uses page content

  • On websites that belong to one of your team's projects, or that you enable, the extension detects login forms so it can fill the username and password you choose.
  • When you submit a login, sign-up or password-change form on those sites, the extension can offer to save or update that credential in your team vault. Nothing is saved unless you confirm.
  • The extension does not read or modify pages on Google's sign-in domains, does not track your browsing history, and does not collect page content beyond the login form fields described above.

Data stored on your device

The extension stores your session token, the backend address, your interface settings and a list of sites you have excluded in Chrome's local extension storage. Passwords are fetched on demand when you fill or copy them and are not kept in persistent local storage.

Sharing

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. Credentials are visible only to approved members of your workspace. To display website icons, the server may request a site's public favicon from that site or from Google's favicon service using only the site's domain name.

Storage and security

Data is stored in Cloudflare's infrastructure (Workers and D1). All traffic uses HTTPS. Sessions expire, and administrators can revoke a member's access at any time.

Retention and deletion

Credentials and notes are kept until an administrator deletes them. To have your account and associated data removed, contact your workspace administrator or email us at the address below. Uninstalling the extension removes all of its local data from your browser.

Limited Use

Global Login's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of data collected by the extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy.
